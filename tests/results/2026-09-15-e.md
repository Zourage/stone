# Acceptance test 7 — 2026-09-15, fresh draw, and a self-inflicted failure

Corpus 1287 sentences: **1162 train, 125 held**. Lexicon 206 entries.
Bar (D50): stone → English 100% meaning-correct, English → stone ≥90% exact, every miss read by hand and grammatical.

## Scores

| | result |
|---|---|
| stone → English | **125/125 meaning-correct** |
| English → stone | **112/125 = 89.6% exact** |
| misses that are ill formed | **1 of 13** |
| real model misses | **0 of 13** |

**Verdict: fails.** 89.6% is below the bar, and one answer is ill formed. Both failures are the corpus's, and **two of the thirteen misses were caused by sentences added twenty minutes before the run.**

## The self-inflicted part, first

The pre-run composition check (D113) named fifteen construction pairs the new held set needed, and fifteen train sentences were written to teach them. **Two of those fifteen contradicted a nineteen-line pattern in the corpus.** Negation on a modal sits on the modal — `wait-GEN-SUB should-NEG-DIRECT` — in 19 corpus lines. The two new sentences put it on the complement instead, and the agent, reading the corpus it was given, followed the newest examples on exactly the two held sentences that use the construction (s0650, s0697).

Those two misses are the whole margin. With them the run is 114/125 = 91.2% and over the bar; without, 112/125 = 89.6% and under it.

**Why nothing caught it.** `validate.py` had no check for modal negation scope, because the corpus had never broken that rule — and a check here only ever exists after something breaks. **New sentences are therefore the single most dangerous thing in the corpus: they are written against the rules nobody has had a reason to check yet.** The check now exists (D119) and is shown against both forms.

## The other eleven misses

**Three are corpus defects where the agent was right**, all corrected:

- **s0508** "Every result should be recorded" had dropped the object marker that makes a clause agentless (D65). This is s0730 from test 6 again, one line over: the corpus was teaching both.
- **s0016** "I asked you" marked the addressee with the object case; D57 puts every recipient in the location case and s0451 "ask me" already does. With the object marker the line reads *I asked about you* — which is exactly how the other direction rendered it.
- **s0813**'s English carried a second clause, "I did it easily", that the script does not encode at all. The agent tried to render both and produced **the run's only ill-formed answer**. The other direction simply dropped the clause. A defective English caused the only grammatical failure in the run.

**One is the question D103 left open**: s0456 "If it failed, tell me now" has two adverbials after the object, and the corpus still says nothing about their order. The agent put the time phrase before the recipient; gold has it after.

**One is the standing true/hold ambiguity** (s0491, D76), untouched for the fourth run running.

**Six are constructions the corpus does not determine**: *someone* as bare person or one-person (s0072); whether an elided second term repeats its head (s0350, s0660); compound against possessor for "the file name" (s0526); *long/short* as more/less time or big/small (s0641); whether an intransitive repeat takes an object marker (s0231).

## stone → English

125/125 meaning-correct. Twenty-four differed from gold in wording and none in meaning: contraction, article, number-neutral rendering, which fixed cue was chosen, literal against idiomatic ("the night's time is more" for "the night is long"). This direction also found s0813 and s0016 independently of the other one.

All eight agents reconstructed the postposition set, the verb template with its obligatory evidential, the nominaliser feeding the modals and the temporal frames, and the conditional. All eight judged the language constructed; none read a word as Korean.

Two fresh-set runs now agree on about 90%, against 97.5% on a set three runs had combed — which is the honest number for this corpus and the clearest vindication D94's objection could have got. **Neither fresh run has found a single real model miss.** Everything that goes wrong is in the corpus, which is the good news and the bad news at once: nothing here is a limit of the language or of the reader, and all of it is work.
