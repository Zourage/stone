# Acceptance test 9 — cross-model, pre-registered — 2026-09-18

Corpus 1434 sentences, lexicon 224 entries, at `9fe7686`. **Serves goal 1** (D125).
Pre-registration in `tests/fixtures/test9_preregistration.md`, written before any
model was called.

**Result: the pre-registered outcome came out in the recoining's favour — minimal-pair
substitutions fell from 12.1% of misses to 1.7%.** Three of five models clear D50's
bar. The defect detector fired on six lines, three of them rejected by all five
families.

## Why it was pre-registered

D127 recoined 17 roots on the strength of one measurement in test 8 — 12% of misses
were a root written where a one-syllable neighbour was wanted. That is measure and
intervene with no re-measure, and D82's rule is that a claim earns its place by being
shown to work. Writing the outcome and the falsification condition down first is what
stops a result being fitted to whatever came back.

## Protocol

As test 8, with a larger draw. 120 held sentences drawn from the 1411 unique on both
sides, so D93 holds by construction; held out of the **context** without modifying the
corpus. Four chunks of 30, each asked in both directions in separate stateless calls,
so no call sees both sides of a held sentence (D75). Context the other 1314 pairs as
`English ||| script`, ~20k tokens. No spec, no lexicon, no grammar.

## Primary outcome (pre-registered)

| | substitutions / misses | |
|---|---|---|
| test 8 | 4 / 33 | **12.1%** |
| test 9 | 1 / 58 | **1.7%** |

Three of test 8's four substitutions were **cross-family**, which is exactly what
recoining could address, and none recurred. The single survivor is `예니` *drink* for
`예네` *eat* — both in the person family, one vowel apart, and they would be one vowel
apart in any row. D127 said intra-family pairs are structurally beyond reach; this is
that prediction holding.

**Power, as stated up front.** Four events against one. The result is consistent with
the density hypothesis and does not prove it. It is reported as the pre-registered
outcome being met, not as a demonstration.

## The defect detector

Six lines rejected by three or more families; three by all five.

**s0724 and s0727 — "takes effort", 5/5 each.** The corpus renders *takes* as nothing:
`이 사세 마소 사해카`, this task more effort. Every model reached for the measure root
or inserted a possessor. Two train examples exist and no third. **Not an error — a
thin construction**, the same shape as the causal clause D127 taught.

**s0377 — the complement evidential, 5/5, and nobody produced the gold.** The corpus
writes `누타카디`, direct plus subordinator, in the complement of *I told you it failed*.
Two models kept the direct evidential but added an object marker; three wrote `누타코디`,
general. The corpus has **two** lines with `카디` against a dozen with `코디`. The spec
says irrealis complements take the general evidential and says nothing about a factual
one — and a complement about something the speaker witnessed arguably should keep
direct. **This is a language decision, not a defect**, and it is not made here.

**s0525 — the compound against the possessor, 3/5.** `와 미네`, test data, is a compound
(D64); three models inserted the possessor. Worth reading beside D127's correction of
s0738, which went the other way: having *is* possessor plus the hold root, while a
modifier compound is not. Both directions of that confusion now have evidence, and the
compound has 29 train sentences against the possessor's 65.

Also **s0214** (3/5, *more* as predicate against determiner-plus-hold) and **s1111**
(3/5).

## A harness failure worth recording

GLM-5.3 returned 0/120 on two attempts before this one, and **neither zero was a
result**. It is a reasoning model. On the second attempt a `max_tokens` cap added by
the harness truncated its reasoning before it produced any answer, and a fallback that
read the `reasoning` field when `content` was empty then captured its scratchpad and
scored that as output. Both bugs were the harness's, not the model's; removing the cap
and reading only `content` gave 240/240 parsed and 94.2%.

**A cross-model harness must never score a field the model did not offer as its
answer, and a zero from a model that scored 85% a day earlier is a harness bug until
proven otherwise.** Had this gone unchecked the results file would have carried a
fabricated 0%.

## Verdict

1. The pre-registered outcome is met: substitutions 12.1% → 1.7%, with the one
   survivor being the class predicted to be unreachable.
2. Goal 1 stands where D126 left it — some current frontier models clear the bar,
   not all — with the passing set's membership shown to be unstable across draws.
3. Two thin constructions named with five-family agreement, one language question
   raised, and one compound/possessor confusion now evidenced in both directions.
4. None of these is corrected here. Today already changed 17 forms, 2 lines and 12
   sentences; the lesson of this run is that an intervention is worth less than the
   re-measurement that follows it.
