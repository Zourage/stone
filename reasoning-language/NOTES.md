## Revised grammar (v0.2 + clarifications = "v0.3", clarifications NOT re-tested)
sentence = [BY speaker] [label ":"] header body "."
header   = opener {relation [labels]} [strength] {attitude} [CALL phrase]
block    = "{" [BY speaker [AROUND]] [label ":"] [header] body "}"   (BY x AROUND = paraphrase, not verbatim)
body     = clause {(AND|OR|BUT) clause} | IF block block [block] | EACH handle IN phrase block | bare phrase / «notation»
clause   = {NOT | time | aspect | modal | strength} [focus] {degree} predicate {role phrase}
           (pre-predicate particles in any order; each scopes over everything to its right;
            they outscope quantifiers in role phrases; for the reverse use EACH)
phrase   = unit {(AND|OR) unit};  unit = [focus|attitude|strength] [NOT] {quantifier} head [NAMED h] {linker modifier}
Openers (13): GIVEN DERIVED RECALLED ASSUMED CLAIM STORY RULE ASK REQUEST PLAN DEFINE SOCIAL HEAD.
  ASK YES / ASK NO = expected answer. OR directly under ASK = "which one". PLAN with non-ME agent = writer
  speaks for that agent. GIVEN covers the writer's own observed results.
Relations (15): CONTRAST CONCEDE RESULT EVIDENCE EXPLAIN ADD SEQUENCE EXAMPLE DETAIL RESTATE BACKGROUND
  ASIDE UNDER ANSWER REBUT.   Strength: CERTAIN LIKELY POSSIBLE (+ optional number).
Attitude (9): SURPRISE EMPHASIS POLITE REGRET GLAD ANNOYED JEST IRONY FRANK (degree may precede: VERY EMPHASIS).
Time: PAST FUTURE NOW UNREAL (+NEAR/FAR). Unmarked block time inherits the enclosing clause's time.
Aspect (14): ONGOING DONE HABIT BEGIN STOP STILL(plain continuation) AGAIN ALREADY FINALLY OFTEN RARE SOMETIMES ALWAYS NEVER.
Modal: MUST SHOULD MAY CAN.  Focus (7): ONLY ALSO EVEN INSTEAD MAINLY FOCUS TOPIC.
Degree (13): VERY SLIGHT FULL BARELY ALMOST AROUND ENOUGH TOO MORE LESS MOST LEAST EQUAL; may stack; a number
  before a degree = factor (2 EQUAL big THAN x = twice as big). EQUAL alone as predicate = identity
  (EQUAL THEME x THAN y). THAN with no degree = "like".
Roles (17): AGENT THEME TO FROM AT WHEN WITH CAUSE FOR THAN ABOUT AS ALONG SPAN EXCEPT DESPITE VIA.
  Any role may take a block. States: experiencer/holder = AGENT, thing perceived/held = THEME.
  Inside ( ), a head may take role phrases: ( risk TO goal ), ( MORE fast THAN 30 «mph» ).
Linkers: KIND (narrows; also content/material) OF (owner/whole) REL (landmark of a relational root:
  before REL x, south REL x, between REL (a AND b)) NOTE (non-narrowing comment; with a phrase = "namely").
Quantifiers (15): ALL MAJOR MANY SEVERAL SOME FEW NONE ANY GENERIC WHOLE(collective) THAT THIS OTHER ORD n WHAT,
  numbers with AROUND/EXACT/ATLEAST/ATMOST and UPTO ranges. THIS/THAT may stand alone. Bare head = neutral
  for number and definiteness. ALL 2 = both.
Heads: ME YOU US(neutral about addressee) SELF (gap in modifying clause; in a main clause = the AGENT)
  MUTUAL STEP n [UPTO m] WHAT (question word only under nearest ASK).
Handles live until rebound, across sentences, also when bound on quantified/generic phrases.
BY block = that speaker's own words (ME/YOU shift); bare block under a speech verb = indirect report.
( ) may group clauses: NOT ( A AND B ).  «notation» may be a whole body.
Out of scope by decision: register, gender, rhythm, wordplay, meter.
Total closed-class particles ~150.
Known remaining gaps from V2/V3: user-defined modals, graded focus, specific-indefinite "certain",
  near/far demonstratives, "obvious"/"modest" attitudes, casual register.
