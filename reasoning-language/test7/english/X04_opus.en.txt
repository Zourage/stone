[heading] Task X04 (namely pharmacokinetics).
1. [recalled] The model with exactly 1 compart describes the body as the uniform container.
2. [recalled; detailing 1] If the drug enters to the blood, then the drug spreads almost fully to the whole set of containers at the same moment.
3. [recalled; adding to 2] The rate of the elimination is proportional like the concentration of the drug.
4. [recalled] The distribution volume (namely vd) is equal to divide(amount of the drug at the body, concentration of the drug at the plasma).
5. [claim; even though 4] The vd is not a volume of the real container.
6. [recalled] The clearance (namely cl) is equal to the plasma volume such that the drug is deleted from itself per the unit of the time.
7. [recalled] The elimination rate constant (namely k) is equal to divide(cl, vd).
8. [recalled] The half life (namely t1/2) is equal to the time such that the concentration of the drug decreases to the half of the concentration at the beginning for itself.
9. [it follows from 3, 7, 8] T1/2 is equal to divide(0.693, k).
10. [claim] Tau is equal to the period that is between 2 doses.
11. [recalled] The steady state means the state such that the amount of the drug that enters is equal to the amount of the drug that is deleted per tau.
12. [recalled] The gap of the concentration of the drug and the level of the steady state decreases per all half lives for 50 %.
13. [it follows from 12] The concentration of the drug is equal to about 97 % of the level of the steady state (namely 1 - 0.5^5 = 0.969) at after 5*t1/2.
14. [recalled; restating 13] The concentration of the drug reaches the steady state at after about 5 half lives.
15. [recalled] Many persons believe that if the dose is bigger, then the time such that the concentration of the drug reaches the steady state for itself (call it A) is less long.
16. [claim; rebutting 15] Time A is not depending on the dose and tau, but time A is depending on only the half life.
17. [claim; adding to 16] The dose and tau change only the level of the steady state.
18. [recalled] The loading dose is equal to multiply(concentration that we want (call it B), vd).
19. [recalled; detailing 18] The concentration of the drug reaches concentration B at after the loading dose.
20. [claim; even though 19] The loading dose does not change k and the half life.
