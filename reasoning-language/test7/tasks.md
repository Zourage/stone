# Heavy science tasks. Each: explain at the level of a strong graduate student, 25-40 sentences, using the real technical terms, the names of people, reagents, proteins and methods, quantities with units, and the causal chain step by step. Correct one common misconception. Do not simplify the terminology away: every named term in the task must appear in your text.

C01 pericyclic reactions: The Diels-Alder reaction as a [4+2] cycloaddition: the Woodward-Hoffmann rules, HOMO-LUMO interaction between diene and dienophile, suprafacial versus antarafacial, the endo rule and secondary orbital overlap, why [2+2] is thermally forbidden but photochemically allowed, and the 4n+2 electron count. Example: cyclopentadiene with maleic anhydride.

C02 palladium catalysis: The catalytic cycle of the Suzuki-Miyaura cross-coupling: Pd(0) and Pd(II), oxidative addition of an aryl bromide, the role of the base and the boronic acid in transmetalation, reductive elimination, the ligand PPh3 and the 18-electron rule, and beta-hydride elimination as a side reaction. Example: 4-bromotoluene with phenylboronic acid giving 4-methylbiphenyl.

C03 stereochemistry: The Cahn-Ingold-Prelog priority rules and the descriptors (R)/(S) and E/Z; enantiomers versus diastereomers; meso compounds with (2R,3S)-tartaric acid as the example; optical rotation and enantiomeric excess (work one calculation); and the case of thalidomide, including why giving only one enantiomer would not have helped.

C04 kinetics: The Arrhenius equation and the Eyring equation: activation energy Ea against the Gibbs energy of activation, the transition state, the pre-exponential factor, the kinetic isotope effect kH/kD and what a value near 7 tells you, and the Hammond postulate. Work one numerical example of how the rate changes from 298 K to 308 K for Ea = 50 kJ/mol.

C05 lithium-ion cell: How a lithium-ion cell works: the LiCoO2 cathode and the graphite anode (LiC6), intercalation, the electrolyte LiPF6 in ethylene carbonate, the solid-electrolyte interphase (SEI), the Nernst equation and the cell voltage near 3.7 V, the Butler-Volmer picture of the electrode rate, and why lithium dendrites and thermal runaway happen.

C06 enzyme mechanism: Chymotrypsin: the catalytic triad Ser-195, His-57 and Asp-102, the oxyanion hole, the tetrahedral intermediate and the acyl-enzyme, the specificity pocket S1; then Michaelis-Menten kinetics with Km, kcat and kcat/Km, and how a competitive inhibitor changes the apparent Km but not Vmax.

C07 structure from spectra (a reasoning task): An unknown compound has the formula C4H8O2. Its IR spectrum has a strong band at 1740 cm-1 and no broad band near 3300 cm-1. Its 1H NMR spectrum shows: 4.1 ppm (quartet, 2H), 2.0 ppm (singlet, 3H), 1.3 ppm (triplet, 3H). Reason step by step to the structure. State each inference as a derived sentence that cites what it rests on, mark any hypothesis you consider and then reject, and name the final compound.

C08 crystal field theory: Octahedral splitting, the t2g and eg sets and the splitting energy; high-spin versus low-spin with [Fe(H2O)6]2+ against [Fe(CN)6]4-; the spectrochemical series; crystal field stabilisation energy for a d6 ion (work it out for both cases); and the Jahn-Teller distortion of Cu2+ complexes.

N01 synaptic plasticity: Long-term potentiation (LTP) at the Schaffer collateral synapse from CA3 to CA1 in the hippocampus: AMPA and NMDA receptors, the Mg2+ block and coincidence detection, Ca2+ entry and CaMKII, insertion of AMPA receptors, early versus late LTP with CREB and protein synthesis, and the link to Hebb's rule. Mention Bliss and Lomo (1973).

N02 basal ganglia: The direct and indirect pathways: striatum, the globus pallidus (GPe and GPi), the subthalamic nucleus (STN), the substantia nigra pars compacta (SNc), D1 and D2 dopamine receptors, GABA and glutamate as the transmitters at each step, what goes wrong in Parkinson's disease, and how L-DOPA and deep brain stimulation of the STN help.

N03 vision: From photon to cortex: phototransduction in rods (rhodopsin, 11-cis-retinal, transducin, PDE6, cGMP and the closing of channels), why photoreceptors hyperpolarise in light, ON and OFF bipolar cells with mGluR6, the lateral geniculate nucleus (LGN), orientation selectivity in V1 as found by Hubel and Wiesel, and the dorsal and ventral streams.

N04 Hodgkin-Huxley (science and code): Explain the Hodgkin-Huxley model of the squid giant axon: the membrane as a capacitor (1 uF/cm2), the conductances gNa = 120, gK = 36 and gL = 0.3 mS/cm2, the gating variables m, h and n, the Nernst potentials ENa = +50 mV and EK = -77 mV, and how these produce the action potential and the refractory period. Then write, in the language's code form, a function for a simpler leaky integrate-and-fire neuron that takes a list of input currents and returns the number of spikes (threshold -50 mV, reset -65 mV, time constant 10 ms, step 1 ms).

N05 antidepressants: SSRIs such as fluoxetine: the serotonin transporter (SERT), 5-HT1A autoreceptors and why the clinical effect takes 2 to 6 weeks, the monoamine hypothesis and the evidence against its simple form, BDNF and TrkB, and ketamine as an NMDA receptor antagonist with a rapid effect through mTOR. Say clearly what is established and what is still disputed.

N06 sleep and the clock: The two-process model of Borbely; adenosine with A1 and A2A receptors and how caffeine acts; the suprachiasmatic nucleus (SCN); the molecular clock with CLOCK, BMAL1, PER and CRY in a feedback loop of about 24 hours; melatonin from the pineal gland; orexin (hypocretin) and narcolepsy type 1.

N07 lesion localisation (a reasoning task): A 58-year-old patient suddenly has vertigo, a hoarse voice and difficulty swallowing, loss of pain and temperature sensation on the left side of the face and on the right side of the body, and on the left a drooping eyelid with a small pupil and clumsy limb movements. Strength is normal. Reason step by step to the site of the lesion and the artery most likely involved. Use derived sentences that cite their grounds, mark the hypotheses you only consider, reject at least one alternative with a reason, and name the syndrome.

X01 stellar nucleosynthesis: The pp chain and the CNO cycle, the triple-alpha process and the Hoyle state of carbon-12, why fusion stops at iron-56, the Chandrasekhar limit of 1.44 solar masses, Type Ia versus Type II supernovae, and the r-process in neutron star mergers with GW170817 as evidence.

X02 gene editing: CRISPR-Cas9 from Streptococcus pyogenes: the single guide RNA (sgRNA), the PAM sequence 5'-NGG-3', the RuvC and HNH nuclease domains, the double-strand break 3 bp upstream of the PAM, repair by NHEJ versus HDR, base editors, off-target effects, and the work of Doudna and Charpentier (2012).

X03 climate physics: Radiative forcing and why doubling CO2 gives about 3.7 W/m2, equilibrium climate sensitivity (likely range 2.5 to 4.0 K), the water-vapour, ice-albedo and cloud feedbacks, Milankovitch cycles, the Atlantic meridional overturning circulation (AMOC), and the difference between the scenarios SSP1-2.6 and SSP5-8.5.

X04 pharmacokinetics (science and code): The one-compartment model: volume of distribution Vd, clearance CL, the elimination rate constant k = CL/Vd, half-life t1/2 = 0.693/k, steady state after about 5 half-lives, and the loading dose. Work an example for a drug with Vd = 40 L and CL = 4 L/h given as 500 mg every 8 h. Then write, in the language's code form, a function that returns the plasma concentration after n doses at a given time since the last dose, using a library function for the exponential.

X05 particle physics: The Standard Model: the six quarks and six leptons, the gauge bosons (photon, gluon, W+, W- and Z0), the Higgs boson at about 125 GeV found at the LHC by ATLAS and CMS in 2012, colour confinement in QCD, the CKM matrix and CP violation, and neutrino oscillations as evidence of physics beyond the model.

X06 a disagreement between two scientists (dialogue): Dr. Okafor-Lindqvist and Prof. Jean-Baptiste Tanaka argue by letter about a paper in the Journal of Neuroscience claiming that optogenetic silencing of parvalbumin (PV+) interneurons in mouse mPFC abolishes gamma oscillations (30-80 Hz). One doubts the controls (the virus AAV5-EF1a-DIO-eNpHR3.0 and off-target expression), the other defends the result. Eight to twelve turns. Show tone (polite, irritated, conceding, joking once), mark what each only considers and does not commit to, and end with an agreed experiment.
