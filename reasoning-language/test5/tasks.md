# Tasks

K01 negotiation: A dialogue of about 16 turns between a tenant and a landlord about a rent increase. Include politeness, a veiled threat, a concession, a counter-offer with numbers and dates, a misunderstanding that gets cleared up, and one speaker quoting what the other said earlier.

K02 recipe: Instructions for baking bread: ingredients with quantities and units (grams, millilitres, degrees, minutes), ordered steps, steps that happen at the same time, a step repeated until a condition holds, two warnings, and what to do if the dough does not rise.

K03 contract: Rules for a shared office: who may do what, who must do what, exceptions, deadlines, penalties, what happens if two rules conflict, and a definition of three terms used in the rules. About 18 sentences.

K04 physics: Explain why a ball thrown upward comes back down, with the formulas for height and speed over time, units, a worked example with numbers (initial speed 20 m/s), the time of the highest point, and what changes on the Moon.

K05 probability: A test for a disease is 95% sensitive and 90% specific; 2% of people have the disease. Someone tests positive. Work out step by step the probability that they have the disease, marking what is given, assumed and derived. Then explain in plain words why the answer surprises people.

K06 graph code: In the logic notation, define breadth-first search on a graph given as a map from a node to its list of neighbours, returning the shortest path length between two nodes or nothing if there is none. Also define a function that reverses a string and one that counts how often each word occurs in a list. Then explain the search in four sentences.

K07 code review: Review this code and report three problems with severity, the line concerned, why it is a problem, and a fix; quote identifiers and the error message exactly.
    def average(xs):
        total = 0
        for i in range(len(xs) + 1):
            total += xs[i]
        return total / len(xs)
  It fails with "IndexError: list index out of range"; it also fails on an empty list.

K08 argument: Argue for and then against the claim "a copy of a person that has all their memories is the same person". Use counterfactuals (what would be the case if...), possibility and necessity, a thought experiment, an objection and a reply to it, and end by saying what you believe and how sure you are.

K09 history: Tell the story of the first crossing of the Atlantic by aeroplane non-stop (Alcock and Brown, June 1919, from Newfoundland to Ireland) as you recall it: names, places, dates, durations, what went wrong, what people said afterwards. Mark clearly which details you are sure of and which you are not.

K10 medicine: Explain type 2 diabetes to a patient: what insulin does, what goes wrong, three symptoms, two long-term risks, how it is measured (HbA1c, with a number and unit), and three things the patient can do. Then give the same content in two sentences for a doctor.

K11 planning: Three friends plan a 5-day trip with a fixed budget. Compare two destinations on cost, weather, travel time and risk; state each person's preference and how strongly they hold it; propose a decision rule; apply it; state what would change the decision.

K12 language about language: (a) Explain the two readings of "every student read a book" and of "I did not go because it was raining", writing each reading unambiguously. (b) Define three new words the language lacks and use each in a sentence. (c) Explain to a learner the difference between two near-synonyms from the word list. (d) Report a sentence that someone else said, inside what a third person said about it.
