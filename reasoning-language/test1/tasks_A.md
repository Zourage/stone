# Tasks

Do each task entirely in the language. Start each task's text with a heading sentence (opener HEAD) holding the task number in digits.

## Task 1
Three boxes are labelled "apples", "oranges" and "mixed". Every label is wrong. You may take one fruit from one box, without looking inside. Which box do you take from, and how do you then know what is in every box? Write your reasoning step by step, each step a numbered sentence that says what it rests on, and then state the answer.

## Task 2
Maria, a colleague, asked whether you can review her report by Friday. You cannot: you are travelling on Thursday and Friday. Write her a short reply: say so, apologise, offer Monday morning instead, and ask whether that would be too late.

## Task 3
Explain to a beginner how binary search finds a value in a sorted list, why the list has to be sorted, and roughly how many steps it needs for a list of 1000 items. Then give the algorithm itself in the logic notation (section 10 of the rule sheet).
