1. Mira and Jonas shared a flat in the city.
2. On Monday evening the lights went out, because the electricity bill had not been paid.
3. Mira said: "You forgot to pay the bill again."
4. Jonas was angry, because he remembered paying it two weeks ago.
5. Jonas said: "Oh yes, of course, I always forget everything, and you never make a mistake."
6. He did not mean it; he was being sarcastic.
7. Mira said: "Then show me the payment."
8. Jonas opened his bank account on his phone and showed her the record of the payment.
9. The money had left his account, but the electricity company had never received it.
10. They called the bank together the next morning.
11. The bank worker checked and admitted that the bank had sent the money to the wrong account.
12. The bank promised to fix the mistake and to pay the extra cost.
13. Mira felt ashamed and said: "I am sorry that I blamed you."
14. Jonas laughed and said: "I forgive you, and I am sorry that I was rude."
15. That evening the lights worked again, and the two friends were happy.
16. Mira said: "On Friday I will cook dinner for us, and you will buy the wine."
17. Jonas agreed, and said that this time he would not forget.
