[heading] Section 1 (a proof).
1. [plan] I will prove that for every positive integer A, the sum of the 1st to the A-th positive integers is A(A + 1)/2.
2. [given] The sum of the 1st to the 1st positive integers is 1.
3. [given] 1(1 + 1)/2 = 1.
4. [follows from 2 and 3] The sum of the 1st to the 1st positive integers is 1(1 + 1)/2.
5. [suppose] Let B be a positive integer.
6. [suppose] The sum of the 1st to the B-th positive integers is B(B + 1)/2.
7. [recalled; assuming 5] The sum of the 1st to the (B + 1)-th positive integers is (the sum of the 1st to the B-th positive integers) + (B + 1).
8. [follows from 6 and 7; assuming 5 and 6] The sum of the 1st to the (B + 1)-th positive integers is B(B + 1)/2 + (B + 1).
9. [recalled] B(B + 1)/2 + (B + 1) = (B + 1)((B + 1) + 1)/2.
10. [follows from 8 and 9; assuming 5 and 6] The sum of the 1st to the (B + 1)-th positive integers is (B + 1)((B + 1) + 1)/2.
11. [follows from 5, 6 and 10] For every positive integer B: if the sum of the 1st to the B-th positive integers is B(B + 1)/2, then the sum of the 1st to the (B + 1)-th positive integers is (B + 1)((B + 1) + 1)/2.
12. [recalled] If a statement C is true of 1, and for every positive integer B, if C is true of B then C is true of B + 1, then C is true of all positive integers.
13. [follows from 4, 11 and 12] For every positive integer A, the sum of the 1st to the A-th positive integers is A(A + 1)/2.
[heading] Section 2 (a proof).
14. [suppose] There is a biggest prime number (call it D).
15. [follows from 14; assuming 14] The set of prime numbers is finite.
16. [follows from 15; assuming 14] There is a number E equal to (the product of all prime numbers) + 1.
17. [follows from 16; assuming 14] For every prime number F, E mod F = 1.
18. [follows from 17; assuming 14] E has no prime factor.
19. [recalled] Every integer G bigger than 1 has a prime factor.
20. [follows from 16; assuming 14] E is bigger than 1.
21. [follows from 19 and 20; assuming 14] E has a prime factor.
22. [follows from 18 and 21; assuming 14] Step 18 contradicts step 21.
23. [follows from 14 and 22] There is no biggest prime number.
[heading] Section 3 (functions).
24. [definition] Biggest item(A) is defined as: let H = A[0]; for each B in A, if B > H then let H = B; after the loop, return H.
25. [definition] Put order(A) is defined as: let H = []; for each B in A, do the following: let D = [] and E = 0; while E < count of H and H[E] ≤ B, append H[E] to D and let E = E + 1; then append B to D; then while E < count of H, append H[E] to D and let E = E + 1; then let H = D; after the for-each loop, return H.
[heading] Section 4.
26. [claim] Before any step of the loop, H contains exactly those items of A that have already been read, and H is sorted.
