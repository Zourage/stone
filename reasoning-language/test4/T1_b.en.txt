[heading] Electric payment.
1. [story] Ana (call it A) and Ben (call it B) dwell at the same house.
2. [story] The letter (call it C) arrived from the electric company on Monday.
3. [story] Letter C said that any person did not pay the price of the electricity for the previous month.
4. [story] A became angry.
5. A: [question; annoyed; to B] Which person forgot the electric payment?
6. B: [claim; answering 5; certain; ironic] The bird of the garden forgot the electric payment.
7. B: [claim; frankly] I paid the price of the electricity at the beginning of the month.
8. A: [claim] I doubt step 7, and I want to see the evidence.
9. [story; next after the previous sentence] A and B checked the bank account of B.
10. [story] A and B found that B paid, but the bank sent the money of B to the wrong account.
11. [it follows from 10] The bank made the mistake, and B is not guilty.
12. [story] A began to shame.
13. A: [social act; to B] Apologise.
14. B: [claim; joking] If you cook the evening meal, then I forgive you.
15. [story] A and B laughed, and A and B are again a friend.
16. A: [plan] I will cook the noodle for us (you and me) in the evening of Friday.
17. B: [plan; adding to 16] I buy the wine, and I will call the bank on the next day.
18. [story] The house is finally calm.
