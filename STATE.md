# STATE — read this first, every session

Current state of the language and the method, kept short on purpose. `decisions.md` is the append-only history with rationale; read the entries cited here when you need the why. When anything changes: append to `decisions.md`, then update this file and the spec it touches.

## The language
- Purpose: asking, code, discussion, engineering and science talk between two people; the corpus is its codex. (D1, D34)
- Script: 74 syllable glyphs as Hangul blocks that spell their own sound (initial + vowel, no final), `spec/codepoints.json` from `scripts/compose_hangul.py`; sounds in `spec/phonology.md`. 1–3 tokens per glyph. Priors accepted in D52 and now tested four times: acceptance tests 3, 4 and 5 and the first drift test all found no Korean reading and no slide toward Korean. (D28, D37, D52, D86, D98)
- No Latin form of any word exists anywhere. IPA describes sounds only. (CLAUDE.md rule 3)
- Grammar, `spec/grammar.md`: SOV, and the subject comes first — every adverbial follows it, the n-times phrase included, and an agentless clause has no subject to displace (D78, D87). One open root class; roots are neither noun nor verb. Verb template: root, negation, tense, evidential, stance, subordinator; only the evidential is mandatory. Arguments bare, or root plus one case marker (object, location, possessor). A pronoun, the interrogative, a numeral or a root before a root is a determiner or compound. No adjectives, no adverbs. (D26, D29–D33, D44, D48, D64)
- **The subject-first rule is right but not kept: seven train lines front a bare time or place phrase before the subject, and four of them contradict a line of the same shape. That is G32** (D101). The validator's count of 0 covers only the two shapes D78 and D87 corrected; the location-case shape is invisible to it.
- Order below the subject: an n-times phrase precedes the object, 9 train sentences and no counterexample (D97). A with-phrase that is a genuine adverbial — comitative, instrument, manner — follows the object in 24 and the with word coordinating two arguments into a subject precedes it in 15, because it is part of the subject (D98) — but D98's "24–0 and 16–0" is really 24–1 and 15: s0625 fronts a manner interrogative, which is **G33** (D101). **Everything else below the subject is G31 and open** — the spec asserted the wrong rule from D78 until D99 removed it, so do not read one into `spec/grammar.md`.
- Evidentials, obligatory: direct, reported, inferred, general. Stance, optional: intend, predict, propose, trust, risk, assert. Tense: past, present unmarked, future (temporal only). Irrealis clauses (conditions, modal complements) take the general evidential — checked over the whole corpus at 0 violations (D101). A first-person intend predicate carries the future marker; the predict stance takes the inferred evidential. (D30, D31, D34, D60, D61, D88)
- Four modal roots share a syllable and one construction over a subordinate clause: able, want, should, allow. (D61, D63)
- Semantic carving, `lexicon/README.md`: knowing ×3, error ×3, asking ×3, change ×3; experiment/test/try = 1. Saying is one root; the message root is a noun. A person choosing takes the choose root; the condition/branch root is the branch point in code. Quantified duration takes the measure root, unquantified takes the time root. (D35, D90, D92, D95)

## Tools
- `scripts/translate.py`: gloss, check, lookup, examples need no key; to-english, to-stone, roundtrip need `OPENROUTER_API_KEY`. `scripts/stonelib.py` holds the parsing both it and the validator use. All three layers have been run against the live model. (D72, D76)
- `scripts/drift_test.py`: the drift test, both arms. Needs the key. (D74)
- `scripts/correct_corpus.py`: the reviewed route for correcting an existing corpus line; every correction needs a `why`, dry run by default. (D81)
- `scripts/regression_test.py`: re-runs every check in `validate.py` against the pre-correction form of all 34 corrected lines, from `tests/fixtures/pre_correction.jsonl`. Run it beside `validate.py`. It is what makes "this check was shown to catch a real violation" a fact that stays true rather than a sentence in a log. (D101)
- The guardrail is a template check, not a meaning check: it catches a broken verb ending or a non-word, and it does **not** catch a wrong case marker. `check`'s modal advisory is narrower than it sounds — it fires when the complement is a bare root, and says nothing when a complement keeps the general evidential but drops the subordinator, or carries the wrong evidential (D76, D101). Read the parser-produced gloss, not the verdict.
- See `scripts/README.md` for what each script does.

## The method
- `corpus/` is the deliverable; spec and lexicon are scaffolding. Done = a cold session with only the train corpus translates held-out sentences both ways. (D2, D3)
- Acceptance test: the two directions go to **different** agents, so no agent sees both sides of a held sentence (D75). And a held sentence may not be a copy of a train sentence, which is the same leak one level up — the validator now fails on it (D93). Check the held set before the run, not only through the validator.
- Drift test: two arms, the long session and the fresh-session control. A run without the control is not interpretable. (D74)
- Words enter only via `scripts/validate.py --add`; run `scripts/validate.py` before every commit. Multi-syllable forms are rejected if they are Korean words (`data/ko_frequency.json`) or if they read as a shorter root plus a verb ending. (rules 4, 6; D55)
- Batches: one subagent per domain with a disjoint first-syllable pool; `scripts/merge_batch.py BATCH_DIR` dry-runs, the maintainer reviews, `--apply` writes. It only appends. (D58, D66)
- A convention that lives only in prose gets broken, so the validator checks them — but **a check that has never been shown to catch a real violation is not evidence of anything, and neither is a demonstration that nothing re-runs.** The D78 check reported 1 while 15 lines were wrong; it now catches 15 of 15, and `regression_test.py` re-proves that on every run instead of citing D87 for it. (D82, D87, D94, D101)
- **Read a check's count for what the check can see.** The D78 count of 0 was taken as the rule holding; it means the two shapes the detector knows are clean, and G32 was sitting outside them the whole time. Same for the gloss-collision report: it cannot see the say/message carve it was built for, nor the D95 choose carve, because both live in `sense_notes` prose and the check reads the `gloss` field. (D101)
- `validate.py` **errors** on: a held sentence whose script or English is verbatim in train (D93); a first-person intend predicate without the future marker; the predict stance on the direct evidential; a marked evidential or stance whose English carries none of its cues (D88, D84, D91, D94); and `corpus/README.md`'s cue sentence or feature-tag list drifting from the code and the corpus (D101). It **reports**: adverbials fronted before the subject, baseline 0 *for the shapes it knows* (D78, D101); English words naming more than one predicate root, baseline 1 — "hold", where transitivity separates the two (D94).
- **The acceptance test is not a linter.** Test 4 cost ~683k subagent tokens and eleven of its twelve misses were findable by static audit; that is what the checks above are for. Spend a run on the question only it can answer. (D94)
- Four places a defect hides, and all four have now been found by hand rather than by a miss: a convention that lives only in prose (D82, D87); **a carve the lexicon states and the corpus contradicts** — the choose defect, which survived four acceptance tests (D95); **a rule the spec states and the corpus contradicts** — the spec asserted the minority word order from D78 until D99; and **a rule the spec states, the corpus mostly follows, and a check appears to enforce** — G32, where the count of 0 was read as coverage (D101). Read the corpus counts, not the prose, and read what the check actually looks at.
- Codepoints: BMP PUA is stripped by Claude's input pipeline (D20); Yi Syllables trip a content classifier on the chat surface (D52). Neither is usable.
